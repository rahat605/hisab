Member balances table update

- Rent = member room rent + current member's share of utilities.
- Individual meal = total monthly bazar / current selected member count.
- Rent paid = payments for that member whose note contains "rent".
- Meal paid = other recorded payments + the existing member credits (personal bazar purchases not paid from cash and cash contributions).
- Due = Rent + Individual meal - Rent paid - Meal paid.
